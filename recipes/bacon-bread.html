Loredo's Bombastic Bacon Bread

Savory, hearty, and completely dairy free, beacon bread is packed with smoky bacon flavor in every bite. It's soft, tender texture makes it perfect for breakfast, alongside soups, or as a satisfying snack. Simple and Comforting.

Ingredients

  • 2 2/3 cups all-purpose flour, plus more as needed
  • 1/2 cup whole-wheat flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup oat milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • Olive oil, For greasing a bowl
  • 8-10 slices of beacon
  • 2 cups stone ground mustard
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • Flaky sea salt
