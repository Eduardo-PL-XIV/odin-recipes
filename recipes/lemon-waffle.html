Lemon Waffles

Bright, zesty, and wonderfully fluffy, lemon waffles bring a refreshing twist to a classic breakfast favorite. Fresh lemon flavor adds a light citrusy sweetness that pairs perfectly with butter, syrup, or fresh berries. Crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, these waffles are a cheerful way to start the day

Ingredients

Lemon Curd

  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/2 Cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • pinch of salt
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 vanilla bean, scraped

Waffle

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoon beet sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons lemon zest
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups oat milk
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1/2 vanilla bean, scraped
